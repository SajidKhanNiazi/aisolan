- Mix **soft UI / light neumorphism** only in noncritical decorative cards or small status chips; do **not** use low-contrast embossed buttons, gray-on-gray inputs or heavy skeuomorphic faux-physical textures. Legibility and explicit button affordance take precedence.
- Typography: sans display/inter-style font stack, strong hierarchies; generous white space; 60–72ch copy max-width; one H1; no extreme italic scripts or decorative readability costs.
